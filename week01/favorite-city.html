My Favorite City

Abuja is my city, an it happens to be the capital of Nigeria. It's an exciting place to live in, the roads are good, you can find lots of buildings occupied with businesses, offices,and government institutions including some great schools of learning. The residential areas have beautiful serene atmosphere with malls for your shopping and other stuffs. They also have restaurants with nice meals and intercontinental dishes.

There are also 5 star hotels and suites for your accomodations, the sport stadium is super amazing with lots of recreational spots. One interesting thing i love in this city is the night view, everywhere is lightened and serene.
